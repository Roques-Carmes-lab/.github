# Roques-Carmes Lab

We develop photonic systems for controlling light, matter, and information, with research spanning nanophotonics, X-ray and high-energy imaging, free-electron light–matter interactions, programmable photonics, and quantum optics.

We are based at the **Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA)**.

🌐 **Lab website:** https://roques-carmes.com
🏛️ **Institution:** Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA)

## Software and data

This organization hosts software, simulation tools, experimental-control code, and data associated with research from the Roques-Carmes Lab.

Individual repositories contain installation instructions, dependencies, licensing information, and citation information where applicable.
